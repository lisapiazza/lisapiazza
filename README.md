# Lisa Piazza

### PhD Researcher in AI for Drug Discovery
**Molecular Property Prediction | Generative AI | Drug Discovery | Cheminformatics**

I am a PhD researcher at the **University of Pisa, Department of Pharmacy**, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, cheminformatics and computational drug discovery. 

My research focuses on the development of machine learning and generative AI approaches for **molecular property prediction, virtual screening, de novo molecular design, model reliability and explainable AI**.

**Technical background**: Python · PyTorch · scikit-learn · RDKit · pandas · NumPy · Classification & Regression ML · Graph Neural Networks · Transformers · Reinforcement Learning · Generative AI · Molecular Representations · SHAP-based Explainability

## Selected Research Tools

The following tools were developed as part of my research within the **Molecular Modeling & Virtual Screening Laboratory (MMVSL), University of Pisa**.

### ADvisor
A tool for the systematic evaluation and optimization of **applicability domain strategies** for molecular classification and regression models, supporting model reliability assessment in line with OECD guidelines for regulatory (Q)SAR applications. ADvisor compares multiple AD approaches and enables the selected strategy to be applied to external query compounds.

**Repository:** [GitHub](https://github.com/MMVSL/ADvisor)  
**Publication:** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.5c01672)

### LogPpred
An AI-based predictive tool for estimating **molecular LogP**, including model interpretability analysis to identify molecular features contributing to individual predictions.

**Repository:** [GitHub](https://github.com/MMVSL/LogPpred)  
**Publication:** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules31152566)

### CDK9Screen
A machine learning tool for predicting the potential inhibitory activity of small molecules against **human CDK9**, developed as part of an ML-based virtual screening workflow that led to experimentally validated hits.

**Repository:** [GitHub](https://github.com/MMVSL/CDK9Screen)  
**Publication:** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/biom16010012)

### CAProfiler
A machine learning platform for predicting small-molecule activity against multiple **human carbonic anhydrase isoforms**, supporting compound prioritization and isoform-specific activity profiling.

**Repository:** [GitHub](https://github.com/MMVSL/CAProfiler)  
**Publication:** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/ph18071007)
